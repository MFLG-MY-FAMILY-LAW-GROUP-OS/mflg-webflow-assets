# UX Summary

This emergency release prioritizes the public Practice Area / DIY Guide / Forms reveal flow.

- Practice Area card selection reveals a connected panel for that card.
- Relocation, Child Support, Temporary Orders, Consent Decrees, and Adoption / Family Formation Review now have issue-specific prerequisite checks.
- `View form`, `Open matched forms`, and `View matched forms` are blocked from the reveal panel before prerequisites are complete.
- Adoption / Family Formation Review is caution-mapped and does not present parenting, guardianship, paternity, or generic family-law packets as adoption forms.
- The forms bridge now uses prerequisite-safe labels such as `Answer questions to find forms` and `Check form availability`.
- Live Forms & Tools verification confirms the same-site viewer opens from `View form` after the form path is ready.
